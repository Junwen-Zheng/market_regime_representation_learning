# Resume Snippet

## Project Title

Market Regime Representation Learning for Conditional Equity Alpha Robustness  
Independent Quant Research Case Study | 2026  
GitHub: https://github.com/Junwen-Zheng/market_regime_representation_learning

## Resume Bullets

- Built a reproducible market-regime representation learning pipeline to cluster equity market states using volatility, liquidity stress, cross-sectional dispersion, return breadth, trend, and sector-dispersion features.
- Implemented PCA + KMeans regime discovery and generated regime assignments, PCA loadings, explained-variance diagnostics, transition matrices, and regime-level market-state summaries.
- Evaluated cross-sectional reversal, momentum, volatility-adjusted momentum, and liquidity-adjusted momentum signals using 10-day forward relative returns, daily rank IC, and regime-conditioned IC diagnostics.
- Documented failure modes and research limitations, including synthetic-data assumptions, regime sample-size instability, transaction-cost sensitivity, and the gap between positive rank IC and implementable tradable alpha.

## Short Resume Version

- Built a market-regime representation learning project for conditional equity-alpha robustness, using PCA/KMeans regime discovery and regime-sliced rank-IC diagnostics to test whether momentum/reversal signals behave differently across volatility, liquidity, dispersion, and trend regimes.
