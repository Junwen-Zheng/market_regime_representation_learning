Market Regime Representation Learning

Reviewer-facing real-data research report

Executive conclusion: The current evidence does not support statistically reliable aggregate or regime-dependent alpha. The strongest contribution is the research process: explicit real-data execution, walk-forward regime fitting, cross-refit label alignment, autocorrelation-aware inference, sparse-sample safeguards, and multiple-testing control.

1. Research question

Can simple cross-sectional momentum, reversal, and liquidity-adjusted signals exhibit economically meaningful and statistically defensible performance conditional on market regimes?

3. Leakage and reproducibility controls

  • Regime estimators are fit only on dates preceding assignment dates.
  • The default workflow uses walk-forward rather than full-sample regimes.
  • Raw KMeans labels are preserved separately from aligned labels.
  • No silent synthetic fallback is used in real-data mode.
  • The raw downloaded price panel is excluded from Git.
  • Committed reports are generated from a fixed 24-asset universe.

4. Regime-label alignment

The real-data workflow fit 92 regime models. Raw KMeans labels required remapping in 86 models, and alignment changed the numeric label of 1315 assigned dates.

The median sequential centroid-match distance was 0.0122, while the maximum was 4.0870. The largest discontinuity occurred around the 2020 market shock.

7. Multiple-testing conclusion

  • Aggregate Benjamini-Hochberg rejections: 0
  • Aggregate Holm rejections: 0
  • Conditional Benjamini-Hochberg rejections: 0
  • Conditional Holm rejections: 0
Final interpretation: No tested signal demonstrates statistically supported aggregate alpha or regime-dependent alpha. Descriptive patterns, particularly in the rare highest-stress regime, remain exploratory and should not be presented as tradable evidence.

8. Main limitations

  • The universe contains 24 large US equities across eight sectors.
  • The analysis uses one fixed regime specification.
  • Intermediate aligned regimes remain partially ambiguous.
  • The highest-stress regime has a small sample.
  • HAC p-values use a large-sample normal approximation.
  • Transaction costs, turnover, and portfolio implementation are not evaluated.

9. Reproducibility artifacts

The report is generated from committed CSV outputs under outputs/real/. Detailed stage logs are stored under docs/research_log/.

This report intentionally distinguishes methodological validity from evidence of profitable alpha.
